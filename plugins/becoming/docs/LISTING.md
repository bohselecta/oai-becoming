# Listing package — owner review draft

**Name:** Becoming

**Publisher:** Corgi-Verse Software (requested; account/legal identity unverified)

**Short description:** Start with what matters. Keep a correctable record. Turn
reviewed evidence into a concrete next step.

**Full description:** Becoming helps you start with an interest, describe one real
example and choose a step that fits your direction, reward and circumstances.
Your saved record stays inspectable and correctable, including uncertain or
contradictory outcomes. Demonstrations you explicitly submit stay pending until a
separately authorized human reviewer inspects the work. With your separate permission,
compatible reviewed observations can support transparent comparisons among consenting
participants and concrete growth projects. Missing evidence stays unknown. This is an
unvalidated descriptive observation protocol, not a diagnosis, credential, measure of
human worth or representative population benchmark. Public-profile statistics are
unavailable. Becoming is independent of OpenAI; no endorsement is implied.

**Starter prompts:**

- “I keep coming back to a game. Help me find one small step I actually want to try.”
- “Show my Becoming record, including what I was uncertain about. Ask before saving changes.”
- “What does my reviewed evidence support, and what comparison is still unavailable?”
- “Help me create a concrete project from an observed gap without promising a rank change.”

chat history, downloaded copies and backups need separate deletion. See
[privacy draft](PRIVACY.md) before publishing any claim.
