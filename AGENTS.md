## Product invariant

**Comparative placement is a core product function. Becoming must tell a person where their demonstrated capabilities currently place them relative to legitimate reference populations and, where permission/data permits, real identifiable people. Every score must expose its meaning, evidence, conditions, uncertainty, comparison population, date, and coverage. Unknown is not zero. Scores describe defined observed dimensions and transparent composites rather than intrinsic human worth. Valid unfavorable comparisons must not be softened or concealed. Invalid comparisons must not be manufactured. Every comparison should be able to lead toward a concrete opportunity for evidence-backed improvement.**

The defining question is: **Where am I among other people—and what would it actually take to move?**

This replaces the predecessor's anti-ranking contract. Do not restore a prohibition on person comparison, a global capability index, or legitimate human ranking. Do not hide the answer behind “shared horizons,” levels, motivational euphemisms, or an easier invented cohort.

## Build and acceptance boundary

The comparative instrument is a complete, browser-local, synthetic-data product demonstration. The separate interest-led layer saves the visitor’s own answers locally; those answers never become assessed evidence or enter the synthetic scores. It is not a validated assessment, a real participant service, an OpenAI integration, or a credentialing system. No real visitor is scored. Synthetic profiles must remain visibly synthetic everywhere they appear.

Preserve the original Becoming identity: restrained editorial typography, generous spacing, forest green, quiet geometric art, reflective language and clear hierarchy. Preserve the original six capabilities and useful evidence machinery. Becoming is the first-person instrument; Emergence is a separate world-facing landscape. Neither requires the other to run.

## Measurement invariants

1. Only accepted, eligible, distinct evidence changes an estimate. Completing steps, replaying a *pending* demonstration, adopting a practice or changing a cohort does not grant proficiency.
2. Require matching rubric, protocol and assistance condition, an active source grant, valid marks, sufficient demonstrations and recency. Independent and AI-assisted results stay separate.
3. The index uses fixed, documented weights. Missing capabilities are excluded, never zero-filled; coverage is displayed separately. Reference people use the same observed capability basket as the subject.
4. Aggregate percentiles require at least 30 compatible reference people. Individually authorized comparisons are a separate permission question. Cohort switching changes relative placement, not evidence or index weights.
5. Every index decomposes into dimensions, capabilities, assessments and source evidence. Descriptive observed ranges are not confidence intervals. No calibrated confidence claim is implemented.
6. Surpass Projects freeze the target person/threshold, reference version, cohort, date, rubric, protocol, assistance condition and starting evidence. Success requires accepted evidence meeting the frozen threshold. Rank implications are illustrative, never guaranteed.
7. Source and individual revocations propagate to estimates, coverage, project status, disclosures and history. Restoring a grant does not erase an individual revocation.
8. History records actual derived state. Compare movement only within matching baskets, modes and reference versions. Unknown chart values are omitted, not plotted at zero. Coverage changes are not improvement.
9. Shared practices require an intentional sharing basis. Adoption copies practices, not another person's competence, identity, credentials, reputation or score. No motive or personality inference from output.
10. Do not use real names as fabricated comparator data. A real participant adapter needs authentication, scoped authorization, deletion propagation, assessment provenance and validation before launch.
