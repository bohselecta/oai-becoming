# Measurement and comparative-product evaluation

**Prospective plan, not empirical results · September 30, 2026.** No population norm, learning benefit, reliability estimate, confidence interval or predictive validity is established by the synthetic demo.

## The questions

Can Becoming accurately communicate where a person's demonstrated capabilities place them among legitimate reference people? Can the user identify a concrete measured difference, choose an attainable comparator and understand what new evidence could move the result? Does the comparison-to-project mechanism produce retained, transferable improvement rather than only more completed tasks?

Keep these questions separate. A polished interface and a passing software test are not proof of measurement validity. User acceptance of a fictional demonstration tests a workflow, not assessment accuracy.

## Gate A — define the construct

For each capability, define observable task families, anchored rubric levels, excluded interpretations, assistance conditions and the evidence needed for transfer. Use domain practitioners and measurement specialists. Evaluate the content and difficulty of demonstrations rather than inferring broad intelligence, character, motivation or discipline from output.

The six capability means, equal composite weights, three-demonstration minimum, twelve-record cap and 180-day recency window are explicit engineering choices. Validate or revise them before real deployment. A percentile built from invalid capability estimates is not rescued by correct arithmetic.

## Gate B — establish compatible observations

Measure assessor agreement and disagreement, test/retest behavior where appropriate, task equivalence, practice and coaching effects, transfer to unfamiliar constraints, source authenticity and assistance disclosure. Record tool/model/time/budget conditions for assisted assessments. Check whether an apparent change results from task difficulty or rubric drift rather than improved performance.

Study accommodations, language and access conditions without treating those differences as a personality judgment. Do not pool non-equivalent tasks to enlarge a cohort. A withheld incompatible rank is more useful than a numerically precise but meaningless one.

Predefine how many and what kinds of demonstrations support each claim. Estimate uncertainty using a method appropriate to the construct and sample; the current observed-mark envelope is not such a method. Publish the chosen method, calibration data and limitations.

## Gate C — construct legitimate reference populations

Document who opted in, what authorization exists, the inclusion window and exclusion rules. Establish stable identities where appropriate without forcing universal public disclosure. Distinguish participating samples from country, region or world populations. Evaluate selection effects before claiming representativeness.

Evaluate sample-size thresholds, tie handling and rank stability. The current minimum of 30 is an explicit demo policy, not a guarantee of statistical precision. Compare fixed-weight same-basket composites and report how missingness changes what the index covers. Investigate the consequences of renormalization and prohibit cross-basket movement claims.

## Gate D — test whether people understand the instrument

Have participants explain the index, percentile, named cohort, evidence coverage, observation date, assistance condition and uncertainty in their own words. Include low, high, partial, unknown, tied, stale and revoked states. Confirm that a low percentile remains understood as a real comparison, not hidden behind a softer category.

Test the complete path: standing → capability → cohort → person ahead → Compare Us → Surpass Project → demonstration → human review → accepted evidence → movement. Ask participants to identify exactly what would and would not change a score. Verify they do not mistake checklist completion, practice adoption or AI output for demonstrated independent competence.

Evaluate keyboard and screen-reader operation, narrow layouts, dialog transitions, reduced motion and error recovery. The current local checks are not a substitute for a screen-reader audit or participant testing.

## Gate E — evaluate evidence-backed improvement

Preregister a comparison of the complete comparative product against an appropriate alternative, keeping task access and assessment resources comparable. Define the primary retained-transfer outcome before observing results. Distinguish more usage, more evidence submissions, improved task performance, durable ability and changed cohort position.

Stratify or report meaningful differences in starting position and evidence availability rather than quietly moving participants to easier populations. Report unfavorable and null findings as well as improvements. The product thesis is that honest comparison can motivate useful action; the study must test that proposition, not assume it.

## Launch gates

Do not ship real public rankings before the supported constructs, authorization boundary, compatible conditions, uncertainty communication, correction process and reference inclusion rules are evaluated. Publish the methodology version and the limits of any validated claim. Maintain direct presentation of valid unfavorable results throughout; validation is about deserving the comparison, not disguising it.

## Software evidence already available

The repository contains deterministic domain assertions and browser acceptance checks for index decomposition, unknown states, context switching, independent/assisted separation, explicit low percentiles, frozen project contracts, review-driven movement, revocation and accessibility mechanics. Exact executed counts and limitations are in `QA.md` and its machine-readable receipt.
