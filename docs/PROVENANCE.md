# Source lineage and release provenance

## Origin

Becoming is an independent product concept and implementation by Hayden Lindley. The original Skills Acquisition Board connected a persistent personal capability context, shared rubrics, authorized practices and evidence-backed projects. This release makes first-person comparative placement its defining product function.

The implementation was migrated from the live private predecessor, `bohselecta/OAI-skills-aquisition-board`, at commit `02a8abe83cc4813bf978aab3498cee5c4ce047b3`, dated September 30, 2026. The predecessor was inspected and left unchanged. Only original source/assets intentionally included in this public offering are republished; no private chat transcripts or real assessment data are included.

## What was retained and changed

Retained: six capability definitions and criteria, source families, evidence-review chassis, independent/assisted modes, unknown states, original logo/styles/geometric map, selected-practice provenance, manual briefs and static/offline deployment approach.

Changed: product contract, placement-first home, explicit person comparison, participant-based references, fixed-weight Becoming Index, nearest-ahead selection, Surpass targets, versioned conditions, recency/deduplication, individual revocation, derived trajectory, expanded methodology, documentation and tests.

The anti-ranking philosophy is superseded, not hidden elsewhere as an active invariant. The integrity of evidence remains central because comparison depends on it.
