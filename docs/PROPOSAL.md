# Becoming: know your place. Change it.

**Independent offering by Hayden Lindley · September 30, 2026 · MIT licensed**

## The product

Becoming is a first-person comparative human-development instrument. Its question is direct:

**Where am I among other people—and what would it actually take to move?**

A profile tells people about you. Becoming tells you where demonstrated capabilities place you, which people are nearby, what specifically separates you, and what evidence could establish a different position. Comparative placement is not an optional motivational decoration. It is the product's starting point.

The home screen separates three things that must never become one number: **standing, evidence coverage, and movement**. The Becoming Index summarizes supported capabilities; a named population gives its percentile meaning; uncertainty and evidence coverage disclose what is known. A modest current standing and a strong rate of improvement are different observations, not contradictory descriptions.

A valid 13th percentile remains the 13th percentile. The interface then identifies a measurable opportunity and a person just ahead. It neither shames the person nor rewrites the comparison to be more flattering.

## The decisive interaction

**You → a small gap → another person → a demonstrable difference → a Surpass Project.**

A world-leading expert may be interesting but distant. Someone slightly ahead supplies a concrete comparison. Becoming finds the nearest compatible person above the user's current evidence in a selected capability. “Compare us” then exposes all supported capability differences, not a speculative story about why that person succeeds.

The project freezes the comparison target and evidence contract. A useful project says: “Demonstrate source triangulation well enough for an accepted mean above this observed threshold.” It can include intentionally shared practices from the comparator. The user knows the rubric, allowed assistance, required demonstration and review condition before beginning.

Completing a checklist does not move the ranking. Accepting eligible evidence can. Revoking that evidence can reverse the change. This is the mechanism that makes the comparison meaningful rather than a reward loop detached from ability.

## A transparent Becoming Index

The release measures six retained capabilities across five dimensions: technical capability, creative production, research judgment, communication, and facilitation/collaboration. Six fixed weights sum to one. The index is a 0–1000 transformation of accepted rubric means, with missing dimensions excluded and coverage disclosed. The same observed basket is used for every comparator.

The index is not a measure of intrinsic worth, all potential, or all of life. That limitation is about the measurement's meaning, not an apology for comparison. Unsupported domains are not filled with invented grades. Future supported domains can join only with explicit constructs, evidence, conditions, weights and compatible reference data.

## People and populations

The destination is comparison with real people whose relevant measurements are legitimately available through participation, authorization or suitable public sources. A public name, a biography or a polished portfolio is not permission to fabricate scores.

This release contains 120 clearly fictional people. The model already separates identity, permission, cohort membership, versioned observations and shared practices. A real ingestion and authorization layer can implement that boundary without replacing the screens or index mathematics. That layer is not running here.

Users can switch among global participants, United States participants, Texas participants, software professionals, creative-development specialists, senior software professionals, people entering 3D development and a chosen practice circle. These are named fixture populations, not representative national or global norms. Small aggregate cohorts are withheld rather than manufactured.

## Perspectives that change the work

Perspectives answer a practical question: what have people ahead intentionally shared about their practice? A selected practice can enter a Surpass Project with its author, bundle version and chosen items retained. It does not transfer identity, reputation or competence. The four authored demonstration bundles remain fictional.

## Becoming and Emergence

Becoming is the **first-person instrument**: where am I, who is around me, who is just ahead, and what could I demonstrate next?

Emergence is the **world landscape**: where is everybody, who is exceptional, who is emerging, and how are populations changing?

Shared capability and reference contracts can connect the two later. This implementation does not merge them and has no runtime dependency on Emergence.

## What is complete, and what comes next

The comparative vertical slice is implemented and tested: placement, decomposition, cohorts, people, comparison, projects, practices, review, revocation, movement and local controls. It runs without an API key or server dependency and builds into both a static site and a portable HTML file.

The next production gate is not more decorative UI. It is a consented participant/evidence service with independently evaluated measurement validity, compatible assessment conditions, correction and revocation propagation. The current arithmetic and synthetic reference are inspectable engineering fixtures, not empirical norms. `EVALUATION.md` defines that validation work without pretending it has already happened.
