# Interest-led Becoming — frozen review contract

Contract `becoming-discovery/1` · September 30, 2026 · builds on main `23b4f4c`.

## Outcome and boundary

Help a person start with something that matters to them, describe one real example, choose a wanted activity and reward, and take a small workable step. Keep a correctable, durable local record so the next visit uses that context. Present one question at a time. The existing evidence/comparison instrument remains complete and explicitly synthetic.

This is authored, deterministic conversation logic, not a connected language model, assessment, diagnosis, therapist, credential or promise of success. No server, tracking, provider, account, new dependency, paid integration or production deployment is added. Publish a draft PR for review; never merge it. Keep Becoming's identity, author, licenses and Emergence's independence.

## Two layers

- The presentation defaults to Today, Your record, and Explore demo. Today asks the next missing question or shows the chosen action. Plain speech, optional examples, editable answers, no imposed coach persona.
- A separate validated local record holds interest, concrete account, user-confirmed exercised actions, wanted outcome, meaningful reward, constraints/accommodations, next step, outcome, uncertainty and correction metadata.
- The existing version-2 synthetic measurement state, storage key, six capabilities, comparisons, source grants, review, projects and history are preserved. A visitor's record never becomes demo evidence or a score.

## Acceptance IDs

- D1: Fresh entry has one required question, no fabricated personal score and an explicit local/authored-logic notice. Interest examples include games, fandom and music without requiring a talent claim.
- D2: A concrete account precedes optional user-confirmed planning, coordination, persistence or adaptation. These describe the reported setting only. Transfer is a separate, untested hypothesis; no score or automatic praise is issued.
- D3: User chooses their wanted activity, reward, constraints and next step. No forced capability taxonomy, timed target or external productivity goal. Accommodations and stopping/returning are supported.
- D4: Returning uses the same saved context. The person can inspect/correct all facts, decisions, evidence status and next action. Contradictory/uncertain outcomes remain visible. Choosing a NEW step preserves the completed attempt and original context; correcting the current step invalidates its dependent conclusion. Story corrections clear old selected skills until explicitly reconfirmed. Attempts are inspectable/removable and never silently pruned. Corrections cannot leave stale conclusions attached.
- D5: Export/import, validation, blocked/full storage, incompatible/corrupt recovery and explicit erase are honest. Invalid data is never silently overwritten. Existing synthetic state is preserved; exports are downloaded only and explicitly include private text.
- D6: Every original measurement route remains reachable within the demo; valid low scores, unknown/pending/assisted distinctions, provenance, comparison conditions and revocation retain existing negative acceptance tests. First-use hierarchy intentionally replaces the older dashboard-first requirement, not its measurement promises.
- D7: Actual portable and modular build, no runtime external requests, narrow/desktop layouts, keyboard/focus, reduced motion, escaping and reload receive executable checks and screenshots. State verification limits explicitly.
- D8: Preserve license, mark, author and independent ecosystem disclosure. Publish exact-head draft PR, verify CI, leave unmerged. Screenshots depict real running product.

## State and recovery

The new record uses its own versioned key. Existing synthetic records remain isolated. Invalid/future-version records trigger recovery controls before any replacement; export original bytes or explicitly erase/restore a validated backup. Failed writes leave the current session usable with a persistent warning, not a claim of persistence. Erase affects only the stated local record; downloaded backups are unaffected. A content correction resets dependent outcome conclusions and records changed field names, not deleted historical text.

## Visual intent

A calm central question, large text input, short human context, quiet geometric ornament and one primary continuation replace dashboard overload. Details and record editing are on demand. Measurement screens retain their full explanatory content inside the explicit demo.
